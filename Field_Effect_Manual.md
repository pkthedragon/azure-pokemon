# Azure Field Effect Manual

This reference describes Azure’s active battle scripts. Numbers identify the field the engine is running. Multipliers are relative to ordinary move behavior. Each entry distinguishes changes to move power, damage, and type effectiveness when those occur at different points in the calculation.

## How fields and overlays work

- **Base field.** The opening scene derives the field from the battle backdrop, then applies battle-specific overrides. Surfing, swimming, or fishing select Water Surface during setup unless a later override replaces it. The engine stores the backdrop field for restoration before applying the final forced-field override. [Opening field selection](Scripts/PokeBattle_ActualScene.rb#L2367)
- **Temporary field.** Some moves or abilities set a new active field and start a separate duration. When its counter expires, the engine restores the saved field and checks Field Seeds again. Splintered has its own expiry that also restores the saved field. Some transformations explicitly change the saved field as well, so use the transition in the individual entry to determine what returns. [Field restoration](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L8694) · [Example permanent transformation](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L9036)
- **Overlay.** Electric, Grassy, Misty, Psychic, and Rainbow use separate counters. Several can coexist and have their own checks for damage, status, and moves. Where the terrain setter uses the full-field route, it starts both a base-field duration and its matching overlay counter. The end-of-round code ticks both. [Active field state](Scripts/PokeBattle_ActiveSide.rb#L51) · [Terrain setting](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9509) · [Overlay expiry](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L8776)
- **Grounding.** The normal airborne check accounts for Flying typing, Levitate, Solar Idol, Lunar Idol, Air Balloon, Magnet Rise, Telekinesis, and Roost. A working Iron Ball, Ingrain, Desert’s Mark, Smack Down, or Gravity can ground the battler. Antigravity Core is a Gravity exception. Several damage formulas instead test Ground-type effectiveness and then adjust for specific effects. The field entries call out these cases where they matter. [Airborne check](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L1762) · [Terrain damage checks](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5188)
- **Seeds.** The engine checks the held Field Seed against the current field when a battler enters and after many field changes or restorations. The field entries describe the effects actually assigned by that routine. [Seed execution](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L958) · [Entry check](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L3567)
- **Move families.** Bomb, explosive, wind, pulse, beam, and similar labels mean the move groups the engine checks. A move in two separately tested groups can receive both bonuses. [Move groups](Scripts/PBStuff.rb) · [Separate bomb and explosive checks](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L3379)

## Temporary terrain and weather

### Creating and clearing terrain

The four Terrain moves and their Surge abilities normally create a five-turn effect, or eight with a working Amplifield Rock. Harvest Moon also sets Grassy Terrain on entry. Move-based setters generally test the saved field to decide whether to add an overlay or replace the active field temporarily. The ability setters generally test the current field. Both recognize a special map-53 override. Duplicate overlays on an existing field are left at their current duration. Perpetual changes move durations that pass through the shared duration helper to seven turns, including durations already modified by the field or item. [Terrain moves](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9509) · [Surge abilities](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L3483) · [Duration helper](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L1116)

- **Electric Terrain** lasts eight turns from the move on Factory or Short-Circuit. Ion Deluge and Plasma Fists can create a three-turn Electric Terrain. [Electric Terrain](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9509) · [Ion Deluge](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L10110) · [Plasma Fists](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L11052)
- **Grassy Terrain** lasts eight turns on Bewitched Woods. The move starts a three-turn overlay on Volcanic, Volcanic Top, Corrosive, Desert, and Icy, or five with a working Amplifield Rock. A new overlay from the move advances Flower Garden by one stage, up to stage 4. Volcanic, Volcanic Top, and Infernal clear the overlay in their end-of-round terrain check. [Grassy Terrain](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9544) · [Overlay removal](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L8789)
- **Misty Terrain** fails from the move on Corrosive Mist and New World. Its move uses a lower saved-field ID cutoff than the other three setters. A saved field at 47 or above can take the temporary full-field route. [Misty Terrain](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9599)
- **Psychic Terrain** lasts eight turns from the move on Blessed Field or Starlight Arena. All four standard Terrain moves reject New World, and the four Surge entry hooks check for that field too. [Psychic Terrain](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L10694) · [Surge entry checks](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L3483)
- **Steel Roller** needs one of the four standard terrain overlays. A damaging hit clears all four counters. **Teraform Zero** clears weather and the four overlays and restores the saved field when a temporary full-field or Splintered duration is present. [Steel Roller](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L417) · [Teraform Zero cleanup](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L920)
- **Terrain Pulse** uses the matching terrain type and doubles base power. With multiple overlays, it checks Electric, Grassy, Misty, then Psychic before checking the numbered field. The Terrain Pulse handler has no grounding requirement. [Terrain Pulse](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L506)

### Electric overlay

- Grounded Pokémon are protected against sleep by the normal sleep check. Surge Surfer doubles Speed, and Electric Terrain can activate Quark Drive. Recoil and crash damage that passes through the shared Electric Terrain helper is halved. [Sleep check](Scripts/PokeBattle_Effects.rb#L127) · [Surge Surfer](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L2244) · [Quark Drive](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L1794) · [Recoil helper](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L2323)
- The overlay damage calculation gives qualifying Electric attacks **×1.5**. It also admits the attacker on Factory, Water Surface, or Murkwater Surface regardless of the usual Ground-effectiveness test. Underwater gives **×2** on the fallback branch when that test fails. Base Electric Terrain and Short-Circuit use their own Electric power rules. [Electric overlay damage](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5189)
- Explosive and bomb moves each gain **×1.3** under the overlay outside base Electric Terrain. Explosion, Self-Destruct, Smack Down, Hurricane, Hydro Vortex, and Thousand Arrows also apply Electric effectiveness. Surf and Muddy Water do so outside Short-Circuit. Short-Circuit uses the top damage roll for its Electric boost while the overlay is active. Rising Voltage doubles its base power against a grounded target. [Move group boosts](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L3379) · [Added effectiveness](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L2088) · [Short-Circuit roll](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L3822) · [Rising Voltage](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L359)

### Grassy overlay

- Grounded battlers recover **1/16 max HP** at the end of each round if they can heal. Base Grassy Terrain and Bewitched Woods use their own healing paths. Surf, Muddy Water, Earthquake, Magnitude, and Bulldoze deal **×0.5** outside base Grassy Terrain. [Overlay healing](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L7112) · [Weakened moves](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L3391)
- Qualifying Grass damage is **×1.3**. Forest and Bewitched Woods give **×1.5** regardless of the grounding test. In Flower Garden, when that test fails, stages 1–4 use **×1.5, ×1.5, ×2, and ×3**. [Grass damage](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5204)
- Grounded Grassy Glide gains **+1 priority**. Active Harvest Moon also uses the overlay for its physical Attack modifier, and Heroic Florges’s Pixie Tribute attack gains **+1 priority**. Secret Power on field 0 specifically checks a Grassy overlay and can make the target drowsy. [Grassy Glide](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L493) · [Harvest Moon](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L4921) · [Florges](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L1074) · [Secret Power](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L5011)

### Misty overlay

- The overlay blocks Crush on grounded targets outside Cursed Field. Toxic Spikes have a separate rejection that checks the Misty field or overlay directly. The common major-status protection checks numbered field 3. [Common status check](Scripts/PokeBattle_Effects.rb#L29) · [Toxic Spikes check](Scripts/PokeBattle_Effects.rb#L230) · [Misty and Cursed conditions](Scripts/PokeBattle_Effects.rb#L457)
- Outside base Misty Terrain, grounded Fairy attacks gain **×1.3** in the damage multiplier. Aura/pulse moves get an independent **×1.3**. The later damage step also gives Fairy attacks **×1.3**, or **×1.5** on Bewitched Woods and Starlight Arena. A condition that requires both Blessed and Fairy Tale at once has no effect. Grounded Fairy moves can receive both Fairy bonuses. [Early multipliers](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L3399) · [Later multiplier](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5231)
- Misty Explosion doubles its move power. Marvel Scale’s field activation also recognizes the overlay. [Misty Explosion](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L482) · [Marvel Scale](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L1692)

### Psychic overlay

- The targeted-move success check blocks positive priority regardless of the target’s altitude. A working global Headstrong bypasses this check. Grounded Expanding Force gains **×1.5 base power** and targets all opponents. [Priority check](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L6353) · [Expanding Force](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L369) · [Target expansion](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L403)
- Qualifying Psychic attacks get **×1.3** damage. Glitch, Blessed, and Starlight use **×1.5** regardless of the usual attacker grounding check. On entry, Anticipation attempts a one-stage Special Attack boost under the overlay. [Psychic damage](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5240) · [Anticipation](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L4430)
- The overlay allows Gravity, Trick Room, Wonder Room, and Magic Room to use their eight-turn setting in the relevant move handlers. Frozen Dimensional prevents the normal room and Gravity changes. Dimensional’s random duration rule can override the standard extension. [Gravity](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9157) · [Trick Room](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9326) · [Wonder Room](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9425) · [Magic Room](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L8139)

### Rainbow, Blue Shift, and weather

- Replacing rain with sun or sun with rain through the ordinary weather moves can create Rainbow for **5 turns**, or **8** under the corresponding weather-rock or field check. The usual weather-entry ability path also recognizes the change. The code can set an overlay above a saved field or use a temporary full Rainbow Field. [Sunny Day](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L8331) · [Rain Dance](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L8400) · [Weather abilities](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L3716)
- The Rainbow overlay can double the usual secondary-effect chance. It gives nonphysical Normal attacks an extra random effectiveness type and **×1.5** damage outside base Rainbow, with Blessed exempt from that damage multiplier. Silver Wind, Mystical Fire, Dragon Pulse, Tri Attack, Sacred Fire, the three Pledges, Aurora Beam, Judgment, Relic Song, Hidden Power, Secret Power, Weather Ball, Luster Purge, Heart Stamp, Zen Headbutt, Sparkling Aria, Fleur Cannon, and Prismatic Laser get **×1.3** under the overlay outside base Rainbow. Prism Armor can double the effective defensive stat there. [Secondary chance](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L7202) · [Random effectiveness](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L2101) · [Named attacks](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L3417) · [Defense and Normal damage](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5172)
- **Blue Shift** sacrifices its user and inverts the type chart for **two turns** while preserving the active field. A matchup with the usual immunity value becomes super effective, and weakness and resistance swap. The permanent Inverse Field check skips its second inversion while Blue Shift is active. [Blue Shift](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffectCompatibility.rb#L1738) · [Inversion function](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L302) · [Inverse Field interaction](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L2452)
- Effective weather is clear when a working Cloud Nine or Air Lock holder is present or the field is Underwater. Some event and transition logic also reads the stored weather directly. The individual field entries describe weather that creates, destroys, or overrides a field. [Effective weather](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L909) · [Weather transitions](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L7174)

### 0. No Field

Battles with field ID 0 use the normal battle rules, plus any active weather, overlays, or side effects. The four standard terrain setters can install a full temporary field on an otherwise empty battlefield. Ordinary rain-to-sun or sun-to-rain changes can likewise install a temporary Rainbow Field. Nature Power calls Tri Attack. Secret Power can paralyze, or make the target drowsy while a Grassy overlay is active.

[No-field backdrop fallback](Scripts/PokeBattle_ActualScene.rb#L2365) · [Terrain setters](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L9509) · [Rainbow setter](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L8348) · [Secret Power secondary](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L5011) · [Nature Power fallback](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L5760)

### 1. Electric Terrain

- Electric attacks get ×1.5 when the user passes the Ground-effectiveness check. This base-field boost requires the Electric Terrain overlay timer to be zero. The ordinary Electric overlay damage bonus also excludes this base field. Explosive moves and bomb moves each have a separate ×1.5 clause. Explosion, Self-Destruct, Smack Down, Surf, Muddy Water, Hurricane, Thousand Arrows, and Hydro Vortex also gain an Electric matchup component. Galvanize's branch uses a ×1.5 conversion boost. ### 50. Library Field

- **Damage.** Confused targets take a ×2 damage modifier. Fire moves, special Psychic moves, slashing or wind moves, and Focus Punch, Focus Blast, Return, Frustration, and Perforate have separate ×1.3 checks. Smart Strike, Ancient Power, Barrage, Headbutt, Zen Headbutt, Iron Head, Head Smash, Head Charge, Skull Bash, and Mind Blown get ×1.5. Fire Spin and Whirlpool get both ×1.5 in the power calculation and another ×1.5 to damage. [Power calculation](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L2998) · [Second trap multiplier](Scripts/PokeBattle_Move.rb#L5782)
- **Targeting and move effects.** Fire Spin, Mean Look, Ominous Wind, Inferno, and Whirlpool target all opposing Pokémon. Focus Blast bypasses the ordinary accuracy roll, and damage received earlier that turn does not interrupt Focus Punch. Focus Energy sets the critical-hit effect to 3, Calm Mind raises both special stats by 2, Amnesia raises Special Defense by 3, and Helping Hand gives ×2. Roar, Whirlwind, and Telekinesis deal ⅙ maximum HP in their Library branches, before their remaining effects resolve. An eligible user of a sound move gets 3 turns of the Throat Chop effect, which halves its Speed in Library. ## Field-dependent moves and Mimicry

- **Nature Power** uses the active numbered field. On Dragon’s Den, a matching value in the battle’s separate base-field property can change its default Dragon Pulse into Dark Pulse (Dark Crystal), Flamethrower (Volcanic), Ice Beam (Icy), Rock Tomb (Cave), Power Gem (Crystal Cavern), or Gunk Shot (Corrupted Cave). The constructor initializes that property to 0. [Nature Power executor](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L5647)
- **Camouflage** attempts to replace the user’s primary type and retypes its currently Normal battle moves to the new type. **Mimicry** sets both types on entry and checks them again at round end. In both paths a Normal result has numeric type 0 and is skipped. On New World, a Normal roll therefore leaves the current type in place. [Camouflage executor](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L3299) · [Entry Mimicry](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L3925) · [Round-end Mimicry](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battle.rb#L8973)
- **Color Change** has its own entry table. It sets both types and can choose Normal. If the current field has no entry in that table, it checks Electric, Grassy, Misty, and Psychic overlays in that order, then uses the map environment. For example, it chooses Electric on Beach, Bug on ID 47, and Flying on ID 48. Those results belong to Color Change’s entry hook. [Color Change field typing](Scripts/PokeBattle_Battler.rb#L1559)
- **Secret Power** remains Secret Power. The selected move in its animation table only supplies the animation. The secondary column below is the branch used if its secondary effect fires. A faulty branch can reach a missing-argument call or an undefined receiver after passing its eligibility checks. [Secret Power executor](Scripts/PokeBattle_MoveEffects.rb#L4900) · [Status method signatures](Scripts/PokeBattle_Effects.rb#L136)
