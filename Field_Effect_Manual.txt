#===============================================================#
General
#===============================================================#
*	Base field. The opening scene derives the field from the battle backdrop, then applies
	battle-specific overrides. Surfing, swimming, or fishing select Water Surface during setup unless a
	later override replaces it. The engine stores the backdrop field for restoration before applying the
	final forced-field override.
*	Temporary field. Some moves or abilities set a new active field and start a separate duration. When
	its counter expires, the engine restores the saved field and checks Field Seeds again. Splintered
	has its own expiry that also restores the saved field. Some transformations explicitly change the
	saved field as well, so use the transition in the individual entry to determine what returns.
*	Overlay. Electric, Grassy, Misty, Psychic, and Rainbow use separate counters. Several can coexist
	and have their own checks for damage, status, and moves. Where the terrain setter uses the
	full-field route, it starts both a base-field duration and its matching overlay counter. The
	end-of-round code ticks both.
*	Grounding. The normal airborne check accounts for Flying typing, Levitate, Solar Idol, Lunar Idol,
	Air Balloon, Magnet Rise, Telekinesis, and Roost. A working Iron Ball, Ingrain, Desert's Mark, Smack
	Down, or Gravity can ground the battler. Antigravity Core is a Gravity exception. Several damage
	formulas instead test Ground-type effectiveness and then adjust for specific effects. The field
	entries call out these cases where they matter.
*	Seeds. The engine checks the held Field Seed against the current field when a battler enters and
	after many field changes or restorations. The field entries describe the effects actually assigned
	by that routine.
*	Move families. Bomb, explosive, wind, pulse, beam, and similar labels mean the move groups the
	engine checks. A move in two separately tested groups can receive both bonuses.

(Temporary terrain and weather):
*	The four Terrain moves and their Surge abilities normally create a five-turn effect, or eight with
	a working Amplifield Rock. Harvest Moon also sets Grassy Terrain on entry. Move-based setters
	generally test the saved field to decide whether to add an overlay or replace the active field
	temporarily. The ability setters generally test the current field. Both recognize a special map-53
	override. Duplicate overlays on an existing field are left at their current duration. Perpetual
	changes move durations that pass through the shared duration helper to seven turns, including
	durations already modified by the field or item.
*	Steel Roller needs one of the four standard terrain overlays. A damaging hit clears all four
	counters. Teraform Zero clears weather and the four overlays and restores the saved field when a
	temporary full-field or Splintered duration is present.
*	Terrain Pulse uses the matching terrain type and doubles base power. With multiple overlays, it
	checks Electric, Grassy, Misty, then Psychic before checking the numbered field. The Terrain Pulse
	handler has no grounding requirement.
*	Blue Shift sacrifices its user and inverts the type chart for two turns while preserving the active
	field. A matchup with the usual immunity value becomes super effective, and weakness and resistance
	swap. The permanent Inverse Field check skips its second inversion while Blue Shift is active.
*	Effective weather is clear when a working Cloud Nine or Air Lock holder is present or the field is
	Underwater. Some event and transition logic also reads the stored weather directly. The individual
	field entries describe weather that creates, destroys, or overrides a field.

(Field-dependent moves and abilities):
*	Nature Power uses the active numbered field. On Dragon's Den, a matching value in the battle's
	separate base-field property can change its default Dragon Pulse into Dark Pulse (Dark Crystal),
	Flamethrower (Volcanic), Ice Beam (Icy), Rock Tomb (Cave), Power Gem (Crystal Cavern), or Gunk Shot
	(Corrupted Cave). The constructor initializes that property to 0.
*	Camouflage attempts to replace the user's primary type and retypes its currently Normal battle
	moves to the new type. Mimicry sets both types on entry and checks them again at round end. In both
	paths a Normal result has numeric type 0 and is skipped. On New World, a Normal roll therefore
	leaves the current type in place.
*	Color Change has its own entry table. It sets both types and can choose Normal. If the current
	field has no entry in that table, it checks Electric, Grassy, Misty, and Psychic overlays in that
	order, then uses the map environment. For example, it chooses Electric on Beach, Bug on ID 47, and
	Flying on ID 48. Those results belong to Color Change's entry hook.
*	Secret Power remains Secret Power. The selected move in its animation table only supplies the
	animation. The secondary listed for each field is the branch used if its secondary effect fires. A
	faulty branch can reach a missing-argument call or an undefined receiver after passing its
	eligibility checks.

#===============================================================#
Field Effect Listing
#===============================================================#

#------------------------- 0. No Field -------------------------#
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	Battles with field ID 0 use the normal battle rules, plus any active weather, overlays, or side
	effects.
(The field can be created by):
*	The four standard terrain setters can install a full temporary field on an otherwise empty
	battlefield.
*	Ordinary rain-to-sun or sun-to-rain changes can likewise install a temporary Rainbow Field.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Nature Power calls Tri Attack.
*	Secret Power can paralyze, or make the target drowsy while a Grassy overlay is active.
*	Camouflage leaves the user unchanged.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Mimicry leaves the holder unchanged.



#------------------- 1. Electric Terrain [ET] ------------------#
"The field is hyper-charged!"
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	Grounded Pokémon are protected from sleep and Yawn.
(The field can be created by):
*	Electric Terrain lasts eight turns from the move on Factory or Short-Circuit. Ion Deluge and Plasma
	Fists can create a three-turn Electric Terrain.
*	Electric Surge sets Electric Terrain on entry.
(Overlay):
Electric Terrain:
*	Grounded Pokémon are protected against sleep by the normal sleep check. Surge Surfer doubles Speed,
	and Electric Terrain can activate Quark Drive. Recoil and crash damage that passes through the
	shared Electric Terrain helper is halved.
*	The overlay damage calculation gives qualifying Electric attacks x1.5. It also admits the attacker
	on Factory, Water Surface, or Murkwater Surface regardless of the usual Ground-effectiveness test.
	Underwater gives x2 on the fallback branch when that test fails. Base Electric Terrain and
	Short-Circuit use their own Electric power rules.
*	Explosive and bomb moves each gain x1.3 under the overlay outside base Electric Terrain. Explosion,
	Self-Destruct, Smack Down, Hurricane, Hydro Vortex, and Thousand Arrows also apply Electric
	effectiveness. Surf and Muddy Water do so outside Short-Circuit. Short-Circuit uses the top damage
	roll for its Electric boost while the overlay is active. Rising Voltage doubles its base power
	against a grounded target.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Electric attacks get x1.5 when the user passes the Ground-effectiveness check. This base-field
	boost requires the Electric Terrain overlay timer to be zero.
*	The ordinary Electric overlay damage bonus also excludes this base field.
*	Explosive moves and bomb moves each have a separate x1.5 clause.
*	Explosion, Self-Destruct, Smack Down, Surf, Muddy Water, Hurricane, Thousand Arrows, and Hydro
	Vortex also gain an Electric matchup component.
*	Rising Voltage doubles base power against a grounded target.
*	Charge gives +2 Special Defense, Eerie Impulse lowers Special Attack by three stages, Magnet Rise
	has an eight-turn base duration, and Electrogenesis restores two-thirds of max HP.
*	The Focus Punch effect fails its focus check.
*	Nature Power calls Thunderbolt.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Electric.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Paralysis.
(The following moves change this field):
*	Tectonic Rage clears the field, restoring the previous field if different.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Galvanize's branch uses a x1.5 conversion boost.
*	Surge Surfer doubles Speed, Quark Drive activates, and the electric recoil/crash helper sets
	qualifying damage to zero.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Electric.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Elemental Seed gives +1 Defense and Charge.



#-------------------- 2. Grassy Terrain [GT] -------------------#
"The field is in full bloom."
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	Eligible grounded battlers heal one-sixteenth of max HP at round end.
(The field can be created by):
*	Grassy Terrain lasts eight turns on Bewitched Woods. The move starts a three-turn overlay on
	Volcanic, Volcanic Top, Corrosive, Desert, and Icy, or five with a working Amplifield Rock. A new
	overlay from the move advances Flower Garden by one stage, up to stage 4. Volcanic, Volcanic Top,
	and Infernal clear the overlay in their end-of-round terrain check.
*	Grassy Surge and Harvest Moon set Grassy Terrain on entry.
(Overlay):
Grassy Terrain:
*	Grounded battlers recover 1/16 max HP at the end of each round if they can heal. Base Grassy
	Terrain and Bewitched Woods use their own healing paths. Surf, Muddy Water, Earthquake, Magnitude,
	and Bulldoze deal x0.5 outside base Grassy Terrain.
*	Qualifying Grass damage is x1.3. Forest and Bewitched Woods give x1.5 regardless of the grounding
	test. In Flower Garden, when that test fails, stages 1-4 use x1.5, x1.5, x2, and x3.
*	Grounded Grassy Glide gains +1 priority. Active Harvest Moon also uses the overlay for its physical
	Attack modifier, and Heroic Florges's Pixie Tribute attack gains +1 priority. Secret Power on field
	0 specifically checks a Grassy overlay and can make the target drowsy.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Grass attacks get x1.5 for users that pass the Ground-effectiveness check when the same overlay is
	absent.
*	Fire damage is x1.5 against targets that pass that check.
*	Wind moves get x1.5.
*	Surf, Muddy Water, Earthquake, Magnitude, and Bulldoze get x0.5.
*	Sludge Wave and Acid Downpour get a separate x1.3 clause.
*	Snap Trap takes one-sixth of the trapped target's max HP per field-specific tick.
*	Coil and Growth use two-stage boosts.
*	Floral Healing restores two-thirds of the target's max HP.
*	Nature's Madness resolves for 1 HP of fixed damage on a successful ordinary hit because the field
	branch uses integer division.
*	Grounded Grassy Glide gains +1 priority.
*	Nature Power calls Energy Ball.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Grass.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Drowsiness.
(The following moves change this field):
*	Acid Downpour creates temporary Corrosive Field.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Cotton Down attempts a two-stage Speed drop.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Grass.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Big Root multiplies this recovery by 1.3.
*	Elemental Seed gives +1 Defense and Ingrain.



#-------------------- 3. Misty Terrain [MT] --------------------#
"Mist settles on the field."
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	Grounded battlers are protected from major status and Crush through their standard checks.
(The field can be created by):
*	Misty Terrain fails from the move on Corrosive Mist and New World. Its move uses a lower
	saved-field ID cutoff than the other three setters. A saved field at 47 or above can take the
	temporary full-field route.
*	Misty Surge sets Misty Terrain on entry.
(Overlay):
Misty Terrain:
*	The overlay blocks Crush on grounded targets outside Cursed Field. Toxic Spikes have a separate
	rejection that checks the Misty field or overlay directly. The common major-status protection checks
	numbered field 3.
*	Outside base Misty Terrain, grounded Fairy attacks gain x1.3 in the damage multiplier. Aura/pulse
	moves get an independent x1.3. The later damage step also gives Fairy attacks x1.3, or x1.5 on
	Bewitched Woods and Starlight Arena. A condition that requires both Blessed and Fairy Tale at once
	has no effect. Grounded Fairy moves can receive both Fairy bonuses.
*	Misty Explosion doubles its move power. Marvel Scale's field activation also recognizes the
	overlay.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Dragon damage is x0.5.
*	Fairy damage is x1.5 when the Misty Terrain overlay is absent.
*	Pulse moves, Mist Ball, Steam Eruption, Strange Steam, Clear Smog, and Smog get x1.5.
*	Hidden Power and the listed variants, Secret Power, Ancient Power, and Stored Power get x1.3.
*	Misty Explosion doubles base power.
*	The Toxic Spikes poison check also recognizes Misty Terrain directly.
*	Sweet Kiss has 100 base accuracy.
*	Defog also lowers Defense and Special Defense by one stage.
*	The Haze and Mist handlers can raise both defenses on the user and allies.
*	Aromatic Mist raises both defenses on its recipients.
*	Nature Power calls Mist Ball.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Fairy.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Special Attack -1.
(The following stats are affected by this field):
*	The Fairy Special Defense calculation gets x1.5.
(The following moves change this field):
*	Wind moves in the clearing list include Gust, Whirlwind, Razor Wind, Hurricane, Defog, Twister,
	Tailwind, and Supersonic Skystrike.
*	The clearing routine restores the backed-up field or clears to No Field.
*	Acid Downpour creates temporary Corrosive Mist.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Pastel Veil can halve Poison attack offense against its holder or a protected ally.
*	Pixilate's branch uses a x1.5 conversion boost.
*	Marvel Scale can activate with the field alone.
*	Dry Skin can heal one-sixteenth max HP at round end, and Aqua Ring recovery doubles.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Fairy.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Elemental Seed gives +1 Special Defense, heals the holder, and clears its major status.



#----------------- 4. Dark Crystal Cavern [DCC] ----------------#
"Darkness is gathering..."
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Dark Pulse, Night Daze, Night Slash, Shadow Ball, Shadow Punch, Shadow Claw, Shadow Sneak, Shadow
	Force, Shadow Bone, and Black Hole Eclipse get x1.5.

	Purge, Dazzling Gleam, Mirror Shot, Techno Blast, Doom Dummy, Power Gem, Moongeist Beam, and
	Menacing Moonraze Maelstrom.
*	Prismatic Laser gets x2, and Earthquake, Bulldoze, Magnitude, and Tectonic Rage gain a separate
	x1.3 clause.
*	Dark Void has 100 base accuracy.
*	Flash uses the enhanced accuracy drop.
*	The target success check blocks the Solar Beam-class function while the field is active.
*	Nature Power calls Dark Pulse.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Dark.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Accuracy -1.
(The following moves change this field):
*	Sunny Day and Drought change the field into Crystal Cavern.
*	The ordinary Earthquake, Bulldoze, Magnitude, and Tectonic Rage field branch cracks the crystals
	once and converts to Cave at two.
*	The Tectonic Rage Z-Move handler also has a direct change to Cave.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Prism Armor and Shadow Shield double the defensive multiplier.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Dark.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Magical Seed gives +1 Special Defense and Magic Coat.



#--------------------- 5. Chess Board [CB] ---------------------#
"Opening variation set."
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Strength, Ancient Power, Continental Crush, Psychic, and Rock Throw get x1.5.
*	Barrage gets x2. These groups are halved against targets with Adaptability, Anticipation,
	Synchronize, or Telepathy and doubled against targets with Oblivious, Klutz, Defeatist, Unaware,
	Simple, confusion, or the Fairy Silvally effect.
*	Telekinesis can add another x2.
*	Strength, Psychic, and Barrage gain a Rock matchup component.
*	Feint, Feint Attack, Fake Out, Sucker Punch, and Gilded Arrow get x1.5, with Gilded Arrow also
	applying Rock-scaled chip.
*	Calm Mind gives +2 Special Attack and Special Defense.
*	The Nasty Plot-class handler gives +3 Special Attack.
*	Telekinesis sets the chess-boost flag, and Trick Room has an eight-turn base duration.
*	Nature Power calls Ancient Power.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Psychic.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Defense -1.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Illusion and the Gorilla Tactics/Reckless branch apply x1.2 offense when their conditions hold.
*	Competitive scales from x1 at full HP up to x2 at one-fifth HP.
*	Reckless or the Gorilla Tactics identifier may increase the critical stage of attacks against them.
*	For an Adaptability attacker, immune type components count as resistances in the field's
	type-effectiveness calculation.
*	Stall can raise Defense on entry.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Psychic.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Synthetic Seed gives +1 Special Attack and Magic Coat.



#-------------------- 6. Big Top Arena [BTA] -------------------#
"Now presenting...!"
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Physical Fighting attacks and the listed impact moves roll a striker rating from 1-14 and add the
	user's Attack stage.
*	Ratings below 3 deal x0.5, 3-8 x1, 9-12 x1.5, 13-14 x2, and 15 or above x3.
*	The impact list includes Strength, Body Slam, Earthquake, Heavy Slam, Grav Apple, and Double Iron
	Bash.
*	Pay Day gets x2.
*	Whip, listed dance, aerial, and sound attacks have their own x1.5 clauses.
*	Acrobatics doubles base power even when the user holds an item.
*	Sing has 100 base accuracy.
*	Dragon Dance and Quiver Dance use two-stage boosts.
*	Swords Dance gives +3 Attack, Feather Dance gives -3 Attack, and Belly Drum adds defensive boosts.
*	Encore has a seven-turn base duration and Rain Dance an eight-turn weather duration.
*	Clangorous Soul trades half max HP for two-stage boosts.
*	Nature Power calls Acrobatics.
*	Camouflage leaves the user unchanged.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Special Defense -1.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Huge Power, Guts, Pure Power, Sheer Force, or Iron Fist replace the initial roll with 14 or 15
	before stage adjustment.
*	Amplify's sound bonus is x1.5.
*	Dancer can give +1 Special Attack and Speed when the user uses a move in the field's dance list.
*	Mimicry leaves the holder unchanged.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Synthetic Seed gives +1 Attack and Helping Hand.



#-------------------- 7. Volcanic Field [VF] -------------------#
"The heat is intense!"
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	Grounded battlers without an exemption take Fire-scaled residual damage.
*	Fire typing, Aqua Ring, Fire-protective abilities, Magic Guard, and the checked form and boss cases
	can exempt a target.
*	The ordinary freeze branch bars freezing here.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Fire damage is x1.5 from users that pass the Ground-effectiveness check.
*	Grass damage is x0.5 against targets that pass it, and Ice damage is x0.5.
*	Smog and Clear Smog get x2, while Smack Down, Thousand Arrows, and Rock Slide get x1.5. Those five
	moves gain a Fire matchup component.
*	Listed wind, water, cold, and sand moves have a separate x1.3 damage clause.
*	Will-O-Wisp has 100 base accuracy.
*	Smokescreen takes two Accuracy stages, and Hail fails.
*	Nature Power calls Flamethrower.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Fire.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Burn branch raises an argument error.
(The following moves change this field):
*	Whirlwind, Gust, Razor Wind, Defog, Hurricane, and Tailwind convert the field to Cave.
*	Surf, Muddy Water, Water Spout, Water Pledge, Sparkling Aria, Sand Tomb, Hydro Vortex, Oceanic
	Operetta, and Continental Crush restore the previous field or select Cave.
*	Blizzard directly selects Cave.
*	Rain and sandstorm have their own clearing routines and select Cave when Volcanic Field is the
	saved base.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Leaf Guard, Ice Body, and Fluffy double the field's modifier, and Tar Shot can double it again.
*	Grounded Flash Fire users can charge their ability.
*	Blaze can boost Fire moves at higher HP, Flare Boost can boost special attacks while unburned, and
	Steam Engine can raise Speed at round end.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Fire.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Elemental Seed gives +1 Attack, Special Attack, and Speed and applies a Fire Spin trap to its
	holder.



#--------------------- 8. Swamp Field [SF] ---------------------#
"The field is swamped."
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	Eligible grounded battlers lose one Speed stage at end of round, or two while bound.
*	Sleeping battlers lose one-sixteenth max HP, or one-eighth while bound in the ordinary HP branch.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Mud Bomb, Mud Shot, Mud-Slap, Muddy Water, Surf, Sludge Wave, Gunk Shot, Brine, Hydro Vortex, Smack
	Down, and Thousand Arrows get x1.5.
*	Smack Down and Thousand Arrows also gain a Water matchup component.
*	Poison attacks against targets that pass the Ground-effectiveness check get x1.5.
*	Sleep Powder has 100 base accuracy.
*	Strength Sap healing is x1.3.
*	Aqua Ring recovery doubles.
*	Ingrain takes one-sixteenth max HP per round from Pokémon with neither Steel nor Poison typing.
*	Nature Power calls Muddy Water.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Water.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Speed -1.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Contrary can reverse the direction.
*	White Smoke, Clear Body, Quick Feet, Temporal Shift, Swift Swim, and Propeller Tail are exempt.
*	Magic Guard prevents this loss.
*	Dry Skin can heal one-sixteenth max HP.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Water.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Telluric Seed gives +1 Defense and Special Defense and applies Ingrain.



#-------------------- 9. Rainbow Field [RBF] -------------------#
"What does it mean?"
(Passive effects by the field.):
*	An eligible sleeping battler heals one-sixteenth max HP at end of round.
(The field can be created by):
*	Changing between sun and rain can create this field from an ordinary base or add a timed Rainbow
	overlay on another established field.
(Overlay):
Rainbow:
*	Replacing rain with sun or sun with rain through the ordinary weather moves can create Rainbow for
	5 turns, or 8 under the corresponding weather-rock or field check. The usual weather-entry ability
	path also recognizes the change. The code can set an overlay above a saved field or use a temporary
	full Rainbow Field.
*	The Rainbow overlay can double the usual secondary-effect chance. It gives nonphysical Normal
	attacks an extra random effectiveness type and x1.5 damage outside base Rainbow, with Blessed exempt
	from that damage multiplier. Silver Wind, Mystical Fire, Dragon Pulse, Tri Attack, Sacred Fire, the
	three Pledges, Aurora Beam, Judgment, Relic Song, Hidden Power, Secret Power, Weather Ball, Luster
	Purge, Heart Stamp, Zen Headbutt, Sparkling Aria, Fleur Cannon, and Prismatic Laser get x1.3 under
	the overlay outside base Rainbow. Prism Armor can double the effective defensive stat there.
(The following moves are affected by this field.):
*	Nonphysical Normal attacks get x1.5 and a randomly selected additional type matchup.
*	The named rainbow group has a separate x1.5 clause and includes Silver Wind, Mystical Fire, Dragon
	Pulse, Tri Attack, the pledges, Aurora Beam, Judgment, Weather Ball, Moonblast, Fleur Cannon,
	Prismatic Laser, and Twinkle Tackle.
*	Dark Pulse, Shadow Ball, Night Daze, and Never-Ending Nightmare deal x0.5.
*	Secondary-effect rolls can be doubled, with separate rules for flinches.
*	Sonic Boom deals 140 fixed damage.
*	Meditate gives +3 Attack, Cosmic Power gives +2 Defense and Special Defense, and Wish and Life Dew
	restore more HP.
*	Nature Power calls Aurora Beam.
*	Camouflage changes the user to Dragon.
*	Secret Power secondary effect: Random burn, freeze, paralysis, poison, or faulty sleep call.
(The following moves change this field):
*	Sandstorm and hail remove the temporary rainbow.
(The following abilities are affected by this field):
*	Prism Armor doubles the defensive multiplier.
*	Pastel Veil can halve Poison attack offense against its holder or a protected ally.
*	Mimicry changes the holder to Dragon.
(The following items are affected by this field):
*	Magical Seed gives +1 Special Attack, heals, and clears major status.

#---------------- 11. Corrosive Mist Field [CMF] ---------------#

(Passive effects by the field.):

(The following moves are affected by this field.):

	Sparkling Aria, and Apple Acid.
*	Bubble, Bubble Beam, Acid Spray, Smog, Clear Smog, Sparkling Aria, Apple Acid, and Oceanic Operetta
